Rotating-blade RCS at 94 GHz

Dr. Nezah Balal · 6.3 seconds · Silent animation with English labels.

What the animation shows

The left panel shows the blade rotating relative to the radar line of sight. The right panel follows its monostatic radar cross-section (RCS) through a full turn. The reflected power changes strongly with viewing angle, even though the blade itself stays the same.

Physical-optics model

This angular RCS signature is one ingredient in understanding radar echoes from rotating targets. The wider radar work combines electromagnetic modelling, micro-Doppler analysis and experimental measurements; this clip illustrates the angle-dependent scattering model.
