Privacy and analytics

Updated 4 November 2026

This website presents the research and teaching materials of Dr. Nezah Balal. Analytics helps identify useful content and improve the website.

Your choice

Google Analytics 4 is optional. Its script is loaded only after you select “Allow analytics”. You can browse the pages and download materials without allowing it. Use “Analytics preferences” in the footer to change your choice. Withdrawing consent stops Google Analytics and removes its cookies from this website in this browser; previously collected data is not automatically deleted.

Your choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. This preference does not identify you to Google. Google Analytics cookies are also configured to expire after 180 days; later activity may renew them.

What Google Analytics measures

After consent, Google receives usage and device information, such as pages viewed, referral sources, browser and device type, approximate location, engagement time, scrolling, and clicks on downloads, external links and contact links. Analytics cookies distinguish browsers; this is not a count of identifiable people.

The site's custom events do not send email addresses, telephone numbers, message subjects or message contents. Page query strings and fragments are omitted from the configured page address. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in the site tag.

A contact-link click does not establish that an email was sent. A download click does not establish that a file was downloaded completely or read. A direct visit to a PDF or COMSOL file is not measured by the website's HTML tag.

See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Basic traffic measurement and hosting

The existing Cloudflare Web Analytics service separately measures aggregate traffic and page performance. It does not use analytics cookies. The Google Analytics choice above controls Google Analytics only. See Cloudflare's explanation of its measurement.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers process technical request information to deliver and protect the site. External services linked or embedded here apply their own privacy policies.

Contact

For questions about this website or its analytics, contact nezahb@ariel.ac.il. Email correspondence is separate from the analytics described above.
