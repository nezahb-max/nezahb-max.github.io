Laboratory overview

Applied Magnetic Systems Laboratory, Ariel University

The Applied Magnetic Systems (AMS) Laboratory is a research laboratory at Ariel University headed by Dr. Nezah Balal, Senior Lecturer in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering. The laboratory develops permanent-magnet systems and millimeter-wave technologies from physical design through fabrication and measurement, and hosts postdoctoral researchers and graduate students. The official laboratory page is on the Ariel University website; this page is a short overview on Dr. Balal's personal site.

Research lines

Millimeter-wave & micro-Doppler radar →

94 GHz radar systems for remote sensing, contactless human sensing (speech, vital signs, fall detection), drone detection, and machine-learning target classification.

Permanent-magnet systems & undulators →

NdFeB helical and Halbach undulators, magnetostatic field mapping, fabrication and experimental characterization of prototype microundulators.

Compact accelerators & IFEL →

Inverse free-electron-laser acceleration with tapered helical undulators; terahertz radiation from short electron bunches.

Antennas & RF components →

Horn, UWB and millimeter-wave antennas, waveguide mode converters, and 3D-printed RF devices, with full-wave simulation and experimental validation.
