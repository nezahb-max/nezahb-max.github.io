Dr. Nezah Balal

Senior Lecturer, Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Ariel University. Head of the Applied Magnetic Systems Laboratory. nezahb@ariel.ac.il · ORCID: 0000-0003-4382-4053

Academic profile

Dr. Balal's research focuses on millimeter-wave and micro-Doppler radar, terahertz radiation sources, periodic magnetic systems and undulators, and high-frequency antennas and RF components, including 3D-printed devices. His work combines theory, simulation, fabrication, measurements, and signal processing, with applications in remote sensing, target classification, speech and vital-sign monitoring, and compact electromagnetic systems.

Research interests

  • Millimeter-wave and micro-Doppler radar systems
  • Contactless human sensing and radar target classification with machine learning
  • Terahertz radiation sources and free-electron devices
  • Periodic magnetic systems and helical undulators
  • High-frequency antennas, RF circuits, and 3D-printed RF components

Teaching and supervision

Courses taught include Introduction to Radar Systems, Linear Dynamic Systems, Introduction to Electrical Engineering, Linear Circuits, Digital Systems, and Radio Wave Propagation for Wireless Communications. Supervises postdoctoral researchers, including Dr. Ayushman Ramola, and collaborates with Prof. Michael D. Tokman in the Applied Magnetic Systems Laboratory. Also co-supervises Ph.D. and M.Sc. students in radar systems, magnetic systems, and terahertz technologies, and has supervised over 30 undergraduate final projects since 2011.
